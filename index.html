Kobo Device Migration Assistant

Migrate books and annotations from your old Kobo e-reader to your new Kobo e-reader

This little app runs entirely client-side in your browser: your reading data never leaves your computer.




If you’re skeptical about handing over your reading data to some website: make sure this page has finished loading, then fully disconnect from the internet before you upload your two databases. When the migration is complete, download the merged database, close the tab, and only then reconnect to the internet.


Built by Curiositry with Sql.js and Lumo.
